How to use this Portfolio CMS

  1. Step 1: Double-click CRM.html on your computer to open the dashboard. Your workspace files are loaded automatically!
  2. Step 2: Edit any section (About, Publications, Projects, Education, Profile & Hero Banner, etc.) using the left navigation menu.
  3. Step 3: Click Save All Changes in the top right header to update your static files.
  4. Step 4: Commit and push your local repo to GitHub to publish your updates live!
