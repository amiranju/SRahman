About Myself

I am a distinguished professor in the Khulna University Forestry and Wood Technology Discipline, based in Khulna, Bangladesh. I hold the title of Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (AFHEA) in the United Kingdom, reflecting my commitment to excellence in higher education.

I earned my PhD in Physical Geography from Durham University and was honored as a Commonwealth Fellow during my doctoral studies. My research primarily focuses on mangrove ecology and management. I employ advanced Earth observation data to map and monitor vegetation and biomass changes in mangroves and urban green areas in Bangladesh, contributing significantly to the understanding of these vital ecosystems.

Before pursuing my doctoral studies, I completed an M.Sc. in Forest and Nature Conservation at Wageningen University & Research. My academic journey began with a bachelor’s degree in forestry from Khulna University.

Beyond academia, I am deeply dedicated to environmental conservation, particularly the preservation of Bangladesh’s critical mangrove ecosystems. My work not only advances the field of forestry but also inspires future environmentalists and educators. In the academic world and beyond, I continue to pursue sustainable environmental practices, striving to make a lasting impact on both my students and the ecosystems I study.
